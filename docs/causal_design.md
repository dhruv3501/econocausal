Treatment: discount level (0 / 10 / 20).
Outcome: revenue.
Confounders: age, income, tenure, past purchases, loyalty tier, days since last purchase.
Why confounding exists: discount assignment depends on loyalty tier and past purchases, and both also raise revenue.
Assumptions: unconfoundedness (no hidden confounders), positivity (every customer type can get any discount; the group sizes 3,497 / 3,347 / 3,156 support this), and no interference (one customer's discount doesn't affect another's).
